## Stage 2 — business invariants and risk

P0 remote discovery: `mdns_discovery.rs::run` admits
an incoming service only when it has IPv4, is not self,
has a parseable `n` TXT, and TCP connect succeeds.
A malformed TXT or truncated name may admit the wrong
peer/name or trigger a panic. Both compact 17-byte and
standard 18+ byte TXT are accepted by the current parser.
Unknown raw device types normalize through
`DeviceType::from_raw_value` to Unknown; reserved bits
must not change valid device interpretation.
For a service generated by this receiver,
`MDnsServer::named_service` must preserve deterministic
service fullname, encoded endpoint ID, advertised port,
hostname, TXT device category and UTF-8 display name.
`renamed_service` changes *only* TXT identity, preserving
stable endpoint/port/host of the registered receiver,
and must not mutate previously returned ServiceInfo.

Note: 255 UTF-8 name bytes is the *parser* TXT budget;
our own advertised identity domain is `MAX_NAME_BYTES=237`.
The parser should accept legally framed remote TXT up to
255 bytes even if the local identity manager disallows
creating a name that large.

P0 live-event gap: current `MDnsDiscovery::run` directly
owns `ServiceDaemon::browse`, `ServiceEvent` dispatch,
`TcpStream::connect`, cache and broadcast. No contract
adapter/seam is available to deterministically inject the
SDK event/reachability matrix without a significant
production refactor. Do **not** pretend pure-function
tests cover socket events, service cache removal or
real Android/Windows discovery. Log an explicit MODE A
testability issue with options; obtain architecture approval
before changing production.

## Stage 4 — branch-to-test map

1. Base64 malformed (already covered) and decoded data 0..16
   bytes: decoder rejects before indexing. Exact 17-byte
   compact path and exact 18-byte zero-length path; declared
   byte count with absent body rejects; device bits 0,1,2,3,
   4,7,8,255 canonicalize. Method:
   `given_remote_mdns_txt_prefix_and_device_bits_when_parsed_then_only_complete_endpoint_data_is_admitted`.
2. For valid UTF-8 names at declared 0/1/254/255 bytes,
   return exact names; for missing-last-byte reject; for
   invalid leading/continuation/overlong/surrogate UTF-8
   reject. Method:
   `given_remote_mdns_txt_name_boundaries_when_decoded_then_truncation_and_invalid_utf8_are_rejected`.
3. `MDnsServer::named_service`: actual ServiceInfo
   `fullname/hostname/port` and TXT decoded
   endpoint/type/name across endpoints, ports and legal
   UTF-8 display names. Method:
   `given_receiver_mdns_services_when_built_then_encoded_endpoint_port_and_txt_identity_agree`.
4. `MDnsServer::renamed_service`: old and new services
   have identical network identity and independent TXT,
   old immutable; repeated names do not change registered
   service domain. Method:
   `given_receiver_mdns_renames_when_rebuilt_then_network_owner_is_stable_and_previous_txt_untouched`.
